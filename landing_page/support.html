Frequently asked questions

A little clarity goes a long way.

How quickly can we get started?
+
Most schools are up and running in a single day. Our team helps configure roles, migrate key data and guide your first steps.
Can you migrate our existing data?
+
Yes. We support migration from Excel, Google Sheets and common school ERP systems, with checks to help keep your records accurate.
Does NxtStepEdu work on mobile?
+
Yes. NxtStepEdu is responsive and designed for modern browsers, including low-bandwidth conditions across urban and rural India.
How do you handle school data?
+
We handle personal data responsibly, use appropriate safeguards and design our processes around DPDPA principles. Read our Privacy Policy for more detail.
